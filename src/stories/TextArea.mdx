A multi-line text field for forms.

## How to use it

Bind `value` to read and set the text. Other attributes, such as `name`, `placeholder` or
`maxlength`, go on the `textarea`; `class` and `style` go on the wrapper.

### Validations

Pass a `validationHint` to explain the requirements. With `invalid`, `hasWarning` or `showValid` it
gets an icon and is announced in a live region. The hint is the textarea's accessible description,
merged with any `aria-describedby` you pass. A disabled textarea drops the validity styles.

### Auto expand

With `rows="auto"`, the textarea grows with its content, starting from `minRows` lines. This uses
the native `field-sizing: content`. Browsers without it (Firefox, for now) show a resizable
textarea of `minRows` lines instead.
