# Skeleton

A placeholder for content that is loading, with a subtle pulse animation.

<Canvas withToolbar={true} of={Stories.Base} />
<Controls />

---

## Usage

- **Wrap the loading section in a `SkeletonContainer`** and each piece of content in a `Skeleton`.
  While `isLoading` is `true`, every Skeleton inside turns into a placeholder; when it turns `false`,
  the content appears in place.
- **Render the real markup with representative content.** The placeholder takes the size of what it
  wraps, so the layout doesn't shift when the content arrives. Pass `circle` for round content such as
  an avatar.
- **A Skeleton outside a container** always shows its content.

## Accessibility

- The container sets `aria-busy` while loading, so screen readers know the section is incomplete,
  and makes it `inert`, so its contents can't be focused or clicked.
- The pulse animation stops when the user prefers reduced motion.
