# Tooltip

Tooltips display additional information upon hover or focus that is contextual, helpful and
nonessential, to clarify the purpose of otherwise ambiguous interactive elements.

<Canvas withToolbar={true} of={Stories.Base} />
<Controls />

## Usage

Tooltips hide information by default and need user interaction to show it. Use them as a last resort,
when space is truly limited and the information can't be shown inline.

Pass the trigger as a snippet and spread the attribute it receives onto a focusable element such as a
`button`, `a` or `input`. The tooltip appears a second after the pointer rests on the trigger or the
trigger receives keyboard focus, and hides when the pointer leaves, focus moves on or Escape is
pressed. The pointer can move onto the tooltip without dismissing it. Only one tooltip is shown at a
time.

The label must be a single unformatted string. Use a [Toggletip](?path=/docs/components-toggletip--docs)
instead if the content should be structured or interactive.

```svelte
<Tooltip label="This may take a few minutes" type="description">
  {#snippet trigger(attributes)}
    <Button {...attributes}>Download</Button>
  {/snippet}
</Tooltip>
```

## Variations

### Types

<Canvas of={Stories.Types} />

- **label** makes the tooltip the trigger's accessible name. State the element's function in one or
  two words.
- **description** makes it the trigger's accessible description, for supplemental information. Write
  a complete sentence in sentence case, under 160 characters, and nothing essential.

### Placement

The tooltip sits above its trigger, centred, by default. Use `placement` for another side and
alignment. When there isn't enough room, the browser flips it to the opposite side.

## Accessibility

- Only interactive elements should trigger tooltips, and the tooltip should directly describe its
  trigger.
- Don't put essential information in tooltips, and don't use the `title` attribute instead.
- The tooltip satisfies WCAG 1.4.13 (content on hover or focus): it can be dismissed with Escape,
  stays open while hovered, and doesn't time out.
