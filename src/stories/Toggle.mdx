# Toggle

The toggle is a switch between two mutually exclusive states: on and off.

<Canvas withToolbar={true} of={Stories.WithDescription} />
<Controls />

## When to use it

When the user needs to make a selection (turn a setting on or off) that takes effect immediately,
without having to confirm the action.

## Usage guidelines

- **Do** always provide a default option for the user (when in doubt, leave it off).
- **Do** use the `description` prop to be more detailed about what the toggle is about.
- **Do not** use a toggle when the change does not take effect immediately.

## How to use it

The toggle is a native `<input type="checkbox" role="switch">`. Bind `checked` to read and set its
state, or listen to `onchange`. Other attributes, such as `name`, go on the input; `class` and
`style` go on the wrapper.

## Content guidelines

- **Do** always be clear and precise in what the toggle turns on and off.
- **Do not** frame the label as a question.

## Accessibility

The `label` is the switch's accessible name; `description` is its accessible description, merged
with any `aria-describedby` you pass. Below 480px the switch moves to the end of the row.
