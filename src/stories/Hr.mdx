# Hr

A [horizontal rule](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Element/hr) visually and
semantically separates content into sections.

<Canvas withToolbar={true} of={Stories.Base} />
<Controls />

---

## Usage

Use a horizontal rule to indicate a thematic break between paragraphs of text or to split a list of
items into thematic groups.

## Accessibility

- **Hidden by default.** Most horizontal rules are purely visual, so the element is hidden from the
  accessibility tree. If the rule is essential to understanding the structure of the content, pass
  `aria-hidden="false"` to restore its `separator` semantics.
