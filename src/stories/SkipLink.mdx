# SkipLink

A shortcut to the main content of a page, following
[WCAG 2.1 technique G1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Techniques/general/G1). It looks like an
[Anchor](Typography/Anchor) and stays visually hidden until it receives keyboard focus.

<Canvas withToolbar={true} of={Stories.Base} />
<Controls />

---

## Usage

- **Place it first on the page**, before the navigation, and point `href` at the `id` of the main
  content. The link is positioned at the top left of its nearest positioned ancestor.
- **One skip link is usually enough.** On a site with very few navigation items it might not be
  needed at all; the goal is to save keyboard users from tabbing through repeated links.
- [SideNavigation](Navigation/SideNavigation) already renders one through `skipNavigationHref` and
  `skipNavigationLabel`.
