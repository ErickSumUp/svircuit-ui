# TierIndicator

The TierIndicator marks features or content that belong to a subscription tier, such as SumUp Plus.

<Canvas withToolbar={true} of={Stories.Base} />
<Controls />

## Variations

There is one tier, `plus`, in three sizes: `s` (16px tall), `m` (24px, the default) and `l` (32px).
The badge takes the text colour of its surroundings.

<Canvas of={Stories.Variations} />

## Accessibility

The badge itself is hidden from assistive technology; a visually hidden label announces the tier
name instead.
